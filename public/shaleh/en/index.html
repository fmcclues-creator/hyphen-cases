Theft Report

Um Abdulaziz (Latifa)Owner of the gold · 74 years oldUm Abdulaziz’s chalet · Khiran · Block 4 · Chalet 42
The report
Friday 16 October at 5:35 PM — Called in by Bu Abdullah (Latifa’s son-in-law). The patrol reached the chalet at 6:15, took statements, photos and camera footage, and returned to the station at 8:00. The whole family is still at the chalet.
Stolen items
An inherited gold set (necklace, earrings, three bracelets, rings) with an estimated value of 25,000 KD. It was kept in the safe in Grandma’s room. Grandma promised her granddaughter Noura — whom she raised — that it would be hers on her wedding day.
Last seen
12:30 PM — on the table in front of the whole family. 1:00 PM — Grandma put it back in the safe and locked it herself. 5:30 PM — the room ransacked, the safe open and empty. No forced entry not on the safe, the door or the windows; the safe was opened with the code.
The code
Four digits. Grandma says nobody knows it but her. The safe was bought by her eldest son Bu Abdulrazzaq who installed it for her a few years ago.
About Bu Abdulrazzaq
Studied engineering in Britain for 7 years and came back to run the family company. He registered the chalet in his mother’s name the day he bought it, and the plaque on her bedroom door is in his own handwriting.
Devices
Bu Abdulrazzaq’s phone (Samsung, locked with a pattern) — he handed it to the patrol officer himself at 7:00 PM — “the family’s photos and messages might help you” — and asked that his family not be told.
Officer’s note: Bu Abdulrazzaq handed over his phone without telling anyone, and we don’t want any of his family to know. The officer forgot to ask him about the pattern. Search the file.
Cameras
The main gate camera is working and its footage is in Evidence. Rear camera (service gate) has had broken lighting for ages, and its footage can’t be exported from the device — it can only be viewed from the camera app on the owner’s phone, and it’s locked with a code.
The family’s view
“A thief from outside, he came in while we were at the pool.”
Detective’s note: there’s no sign of entry from outside. And a thief who ransacks one room only and opens the safe with the code… knows the house.
Your task
Who took the gold? Who helped them? And where is it now?
